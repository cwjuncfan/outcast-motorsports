Our Sponsors

OutCast Motorsports is proud to partner with the following companies and organizations. Their support helps make our competitive league possible. Click a logo to visit their site.

Series Sponsors

Georgia Mountain Fairgrounds

The Georgia Mountain Fairgrounds, which opened in 1978, is home to several popular events throughout the year including the Georgia Mountain Fair, Georgia Mountain Moonshine Cruiz-In, Georgia Mountain Fall Festival, Superstar concerts, and Georgia's Official State Fiddlers' Convention. Located on a 161-acre tract along the shores of Lake Chatuge in the north Georgia mountain community of Hiawassee, the Fairgrounds offers a glimpse into the past with its historic Pioneer Village, an expansive and comfortable music venue in the Anderson Music Hall, and 189 RV camp sites with lake views and modern conveniences.

Georgia Mountain Fairgrounds has been a supporter of the OutCast Motorsports Racing League since its inception, including stepping up to being a series sponsor for the second season of the OutCast Motorsports Truck Series. Please look them up when you get a chance, and if you are ever in the area, go check them out. We truly appreciate their support!!

Auto America – Grand Island, NE

Auto America is a trusted, locally owned dealership in Central Nebraska, specializing in 1–3-year-old off-lease and corporate fleet vehicles, along with a variety of other cars, trucks, and SUVs. All vehicles are inspected by ASE Certified technicians and come with Gold or Silver Certification based on age and mileage.

Because they are independent and pay no franchise fees, customers save money. The business is community-focused, supporting local charities and encouraging local buying. Their staff are active in community events, reinforcing their connection to the area. Auto America invites customers to visit in person, online, or by phone to find the right vehicle and deal.
