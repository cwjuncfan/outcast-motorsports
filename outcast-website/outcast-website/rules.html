Basic Rules

The iRacing Sporting Code applies while participating in an OutCast Racing server. Any violation of an OutCast Motorsports League rule or the iRacing Sporting Code may result in administrative action such as a Warning, Suspension, and/or expulsion from the League depending on the severity of the infraction.

ZERO TOLERANCE policy on any form of Retaliation.
This will result in an immediate removal from the Race Server and more than likely an expulsion from the OutCast Motorsports League.
  • Respect all drivers, staff, and officials at all times — on track and off.
  • Clean racing is expected. Aggressive or reckless driving that endangers others will be reviewed.
  • Follow all session-specific instructions from race control / league admins.
  • Voice chat and Discord behavior must remain professional and sportsmanlike.
