Points System

Point Setup for Races

Upon completion of a race, the Points will tally up as follows:

  • The First-place driver will receive 35 Points
  • Each finishing position below that will receive one less Point (For example 2nd place will receive 34 Points and 35th place will receive 1 Point)

Bonus Points will also be applied for certain achievements during the race:

  • Race Winner will receive 3 Points
  • Pole Sitter will receive 2 Points
  • Leading the most laps will receive 2 Points
  • Driver with the Fastest lap will receive 1 Point
  • Leading one or more laps will receive 1 Point

Playoff Setup for All 3 Series

  • The overall leader in Points during the regular season has a guaranteed spot in the championship race, regardless of their standings at the conclusion of the Initial Chase, however they must meet the minimum race requirements for each Chase
  • If they end up not being one of the top 5 drivers in points upon activation of the Championship Chase, then they will become the 6th Driver in the chase.

Initial Chase Activation – Top 10 Drivers

  • You must have participated in at least 6 out of the 8 races in the regular season
  • If you win a race during the regular season, you automatically qualify for one of the 10 Driver Spots if you meet the race requirement
  • The Points for the drivers in the chase will adjust to be out of the reach of the remaining drivers in the standings
  • The top driver will be reset to 2,000 points, and each remaining driver will start 2 points lower than the driver ahead of them. For example, the 10th driver would start with 1,982 points.
  • Two Bonus Points will be awarded to drivers who have won a race during the regular season

Championship Chase Activation – Top 5 Drivers

  • You must have participated in at least 3 out of the 4 races in the initial chase
  • If you win a race during the initial chase activation, you automatically qualify for one of the Final Driver Spots if you meet the race requirement
  • The Points for the drivers in the Championship Chase will adjust to be out of the reach of the remaining drivers in the standings
  • The top driver will be reset to 3,000 points, and each remaining driver will start 2 points lower than the driver above them. For example, the 5th driver would start with 2,992 points.
  • Two Bonus Points will be awarded (Manually) to drivers who have won a race during the four races in the initial chase activation
